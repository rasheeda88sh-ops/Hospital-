Introduction

Good appointment scheduling is very important for any hospital because it affects patient care, doctor time, and clinic income. When patients cancel or miss their visits, doctors waste time and hospitals lose fees, but a cancelled booking can sometimes be given to another patient while a missed one is lost. In this project, we use AI and past booking data to find out visit outcomes and link them to department fees, doctor hours, and simple rescheduling plans.

Research Objectives

02

The main goal of this study is to understand why patients come, cancel, or miss their visits, and to build smart models that can find these results. We also check how booking time and hospital departments affect patient visits. Finally, we count the lost fees in each department, compare doctors' working hours with booked hours, and test better ways to rebook missed visits.

Limitations and Future Work

08

Limitations

This study uses test data, so real hospital results may be different. Also, the money numbers show only visit fees, not real income, and our rebooking plans use set rates.

Future work

In the future, we want to test this system on real hospital records. We also plan to add more patient and booking details to find missed visits better, and try our rebooking plans in a real hospital system.

Conclusion

In this study, we showed that missed and cancelled visits waste doctor time and lose clinic money, mostly when patients book too early. Our system joins AI with clinic costs, doctor hours, and smart rebooking plans to help hospitals make better choices. In the future, adding more patient details will help hospitals save time and give better care
